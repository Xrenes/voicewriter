Free · Windows 10 / 11, 64-bit · v0.1.0

AltC held

Hold, speak, release.

That's the whole interaction. Everything else stays out of the way.

  1. 1

    Install it. VoiceWriter starts with your PC and sits in the tray — no window on screen.

  2. 2

    Click into any text field, then hold AltC and speak.

  3. 3

    Release. Your sentence is pasted in as one block — nothing dropped, punctuation intact.

Accurate by default

Groq's Whisper Large v3 for sharp names, numbers and punctuation. Falls back to a local model when offline.

Private option

Download a local model once and it runs entirely on your CPU. Your audio never leaves the machine.

Invisible

Nothing on screen unless you open settings from the tray. Click away and it hides. ~25 MB idle.

Your key stays yours

The Groq API key lives in the Windows Credential Manager, shown only masked. Local mode needs none.
