Refine

Fixes grammar, spelling, and punctuation only — same meaning, same tone, same length. Won't rewrite what's already correct.

Translate

One-click Bangla, or open the Translate submenu for English, Bangla, Spanish, or Italian. Powered by Groq's chat models with a faithfulness check before anything is shown.

Copied, not pasted

Results are copied to your clipboard and shown in a clean preview panel — nothing is auto-typed into the app you were using. You paste it when you're ready.

Translations that can talk back.

English and Bangla translations are spoken aloud automatically after they appear — using the TTS engine best suited to each language.

Natural Bangla speech

Bangla translations are voiced through ElevenLabs' multilingual model — natural-sounding, not robotic. Works on ElevenLabs' free tier, no credit card required.

English speech via Groq

English translations and the standalone "speak selected text" hotkey use Groq's Orpheus text-to-speech model — fast and free within Groq's published quota.

Spanish & Italian, text-only

These translations appear instantly in the preview panel and copy to your clipboard, same as Refine — no speech, no extra API calls.

Offline speech, too

The standalone speak-aloud hotkey falls back to eSpeak NG — a free, open-source, fully offline voice — for scripts and languages Groq doesn't cover.

New

Record a call, get a labeled transcript.

Pick the Record wedge from the wheel to capture both sides of a call at once — your microphone and your system audio (what's playing, like the other person's voice). A small, mostly-transparent floating dot follows your cursor while it records, and you can drag it out of the way without stopping the recording.

  1. 1

    Press ShiftAltC and choose Record. A tiny floating icon appears and blinks red while it's listening — drag it anywhere on screen if it's in your way.

  2. 2

    Click it again to stop. A clean confirm window (no title bar, no stray close button — Cancel or Transcribe & Save are the only ways out) shows an audio preview and lets you pick the filename and folder.

  3. 3

    Confirm, and both tracks are transcribed in English, best-effort merged in chronological order and labeled [You] / [System audio], then opened in a dedicated chat-style transcript window. The combined audio and the transcript are both saved to disk too.

One saved recording, not two files

Your mic and system audio are mixed into a single .wav after transcription — you get one normal-sounding recording of the call, not a pair of separate tracks to manage.

System audio is optional

If nothing was playing, or your setup doesn't support capturing it, VoiceWriter falls back to a mic-only recording automatically — it never blocks or fails the whole feature over the optional track.

No Notepad, no clutter

Everything opens in VoiceWriter's own transcript viewer. The .wav and .txt files are still saved to your chosen folder — nothing else pops up.

English-only transcription

Recordings are transcribed in English specifically, rather than auto-detected, so accents and background noise don't cause language mix-ups.

New

A tiny browser, always one hotkey away.

Press AltW to pop open a small in-app browser — no need to alt-tab out to a full browser window just to check something. Press it again to hide the window, press it once more to pick up right where you left off.

Minimal, custom chrome

Back, forward, an address bar, and a share button that copies the current URL — no browser clutter, no native title bar, no stray buttons.

Signed in stays signed in

Email/password sign-in persists across restarts, so you don't have to log back into the same site every time. "Continue with Google" won't work here — that's a Google anti-phishing policy that blocks Google sign-in inside any embedded browser window, in every app that does this, not a VoiceWriter limitation.

Resizable, hidden from the taskbar

Getting your API keys

VoiceWriter runs on your own free API keys — nothing is billed through the app, and both providers below have a free tier with no credit card required. Settings also has its own built-in step-by-step guide, with the same links, for every feature.

Groq — dictation, refine, translate, speech, AI chat, call recording

Used for Whisper transcription, the refine/translate wheel actions, Orpheus text-to-speech, the AI chat wedge, and call-recording transcription. Dictation, speak-aloud, and AI chat each have their own key slot in Settings — you can reuse one key everywhere, or use separate Groq accounts.

  1. 1

    Create a free account at console.groq.com.

  2. 2

    Open console.groq.com/keys and click Create API Key.

  3. 3

    Copy the key (starts with gsk_) and paste it into the matching key field in VoiceWriter's Settings.

ElevenLabs — natural Bangla speech

Used only for voicing Bangla translations in the wheel. Optional if you don't need spoken Bangla.

  1. 1

    Sign up free at elevenlabs.io/sign-up — no credit card required.

  2. 2

    Open elevenlabs.io/app/settings/api-keys and generate a key.

  3. 3

    Make sure the key has text_to_speech and voices_read permissions enabled, then paste it into VoiceWriter's tray settings.

Per-key usage meters

Settings shows a live usage bar for each key — Groq dictation, Groq speech, and ElevenLabs — tracked separately against each provider's real published free-tier limits, so you always know how much headroom is left.

Troubleshooting

Common setup issues and how to fix them.

Speak-aloud test fails: “requires terms acceptance”

If testing your Speak-aloud key in Settings returns an error like Groq speech 400: The model 'canopylabs/orpheus-v1-english' requires terms acceptance, this is a one-time approval Groq requires on their side — it isn't a VoiceWriter bug and no app setting fixes it.

  1. 1

    Sign in at console.groq.com using the same account that owns the API key you pasted into VoiceWriter.

  2. 2

    Open the model's Playground page directly: console.groq.com/playground?model=canopylabs%2Forpheus-v1-english

  3. 3

    Accept the terms prompt shown for that model. This approval is org-wide, not per-key.

  4. 4

    If you don't see an accept option, your account is likely a Member rather than an Owner/Admin of that Groq organization. Ask your org's Owner/Admin to open the same link and accept — one acceptance covers everyone using keys under that org.

  5. 5

    Back in VoiceWriter, open Settings and click Test next to the Speak-aloud key again to confirm it now works.

ElevenLabs key fails with 402 “Free users cannot use library voices”

Your selected voice is a paid Voice Library ("professional") voice. Pick one of ElevenLabs' default "premade" voices instead — those work on the free tier. VoiceWriter's settings include a "list available voices" debug button that shows which of your voices are free-tier eligible.

ElevenLabs key fails with 401 “missing the permission voices_read”

Regenerate your key at elevenlabs.io/app/settings/api-keys and make sure both text_to_speech and voices_read permissions are enabled before saving it in VoiceWriter.
