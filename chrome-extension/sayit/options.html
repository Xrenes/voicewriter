Allow the microphone

Chrome asks once before SayIt can record your message. Click below and choose Allow.

Done — open SayIt from the toolbar and press Record.

Test it

This page acts like a call. Press Start test, say something, press Alt+L to play your message (again to stop it early), then Stop test and listen: that's exactly what the other side of a call hears.

Good to know

  • After installing or updating SayIt, reload your Aloware / GHL tab once.
  • The hotkey works while the dialer tab is the one in front; nothing shows on screen.
  • Change the hotkey in the SayIt popup → Settings.
  • Dialer on another address (a white-label GHL, or a CRM that embeds it)? Open it, then SayIt popup → Settings → Add it, and reload that tab.
  • If VoiceWriter's SayIt uses the same key, VoiceWriter takes it first — change one of them.
