Test it

This page acts like a call. Press Start test, say something, press Alt+L to play the selected message (again to stop it early), then Stop test and listen: that's exactly what the other side of a call hears.

Good to know

  • After installing or updating SayIt, reload your HubSpot / Aloware / GHL tabs once and close and reopen any calling window.
  • The hotkey works on any web page in Chrome: the message plays on your connected call, wherever it is (another tab, a calling window). Nothing shows on screen.
  • Keep a message per project: the hotkey plays the one picked in the SayIt popup, and the popup shows which that is.
  • During a call, the SayIt popup shows where the call was found.
  • Change the hotkey in the SayIt popup → Settings.
  • Another dialer (a white-label GHL, a mic-test site)? Open it, then SayIt popup → Settings → Add it.
  • If VoiceWriter's SayIt uses the same key, VoiceWriter takes it first — change one of them.
