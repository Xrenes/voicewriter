SayIt test

This page acts like a call, so you can hear exactly what the other side would get.

1

Start a test call

Press Start test, say something, then press Alt+L to play your saved message (press it again to stop early). Then press Stop test.

2

Listen

After you stop, this is exactly what the call heard: your voice, then your message, with your microphone muted while it played (if that's on in Settings).

i

Good to know

  • The SayIt key works on any web page in Chrome: the message plays on your connected call, wherever it is (another tab, a calling window). Nothing shows on screen.
  • During a call, the VoiceWriter window's SayIt card shows where the call was found.
  • Another dialer (a white-label GHL, a mic-test site)? Open it, then VoiceWriter → Settings → SayIt → + Add it.
  • If the separate SayIt extension or VoiceWriter's desktop SayIt also uses the same key, remove one or change its key.
