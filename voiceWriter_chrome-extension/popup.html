      Guide

      Get started

      1. Open console.groq.com/keys and sign up (free, no card required).
      2. Click "Create API Key", give it any name, and copy the key (starts with gsk_).
      3. Paste it into API Keys & Usage → Groq API key above, then Save.

      SayIt (voicemail drop)

      1. In the SayIt card at the top, click Record, say your voicemail, click Stop, listen with Play, then Save.
      2. During a call on Aloware, GoHighLevel or HubSpot, when the voicemail beeps, press Alt+L on any page in Chrome. Your message plays on the call, and you hear it in your headset too.
      3. Press it again to stop early. Your microphone is muted while it plays.
      4. Another dialer? Open it, then Settings → SayIt → + Add this site.

      Board

      1. Press Alt+A on any tab. A see-through black board appears. It doesn't listen by itself.
      2. Hold Alt+C and speak: your words go on the board instead of the page, and the whole board is copied, ready to paste into any app.
      3. It follows you from tab to tab. Drag it, resize it, make the text bigger with A+, and Copy or Clear it. Press Alt+A again, or ✕, to close it.

      Dictation (hold-to-talk)

      1. Click a text field on any website.
      2. Hold Alt+C, speak, then release. The text is typed where your cursor is.
      3. The first time, Chrome asks for microphone access in a VoiceWriter tab. Allow it once.

      Read aloud

      1. Select text on a page and press Control+Alt+C. Press again to stop.
      2. In Google Sheets or Excel for the web, just click a cell (or select a range) and press it: no need to double-click into the cell. Ranges are read cell by cell. In Google Docs, select the text as usual.
      3. Clicked into a text box without selecting anything? The whole box is read.
      4. Or type or paste text into the Read aloud box at the top of this window.

      Rewrite selected text

      • Alt+R Refine: spelling, grammar and structure are corrected, keeping your meaning and tone.
      • Alt+P Professional: a casual draft becomes a polished, courteous reply.
      • Alt+H Humanize: stiff or AI-sounding text is rewritten to read naturally.
      • The result replaces your selection; Ctrl+Z undoes it. Clicked into a text field without selecting? The whole field is rewritten.
      • On a clicked cell in Google Sheets or Excel for the web (or text in Google Docs), the result is copied: press Ctrl+V to put it in place. In other text you can't edit, it's copied to the clipboard too.
