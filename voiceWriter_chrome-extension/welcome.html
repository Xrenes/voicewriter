Welcome to VoiceWriter

Drop your voicemail into calls, dictate, listen and polish your writing on any website. The speech and writing tools use your own free Groq API key.

1

Add your Groq API key

Not set
  1. Open console.groq.com/keys and sign up (free, no card required).
  2. Click "Create API Key", give it any name, and copy the key (it starts with gsk_).
  3. Paste it here and click Save.

Your key is stored only in this browser and is sent only to Groq.

2

Allow the microphone

Checking…

Chrome asks once. After that, dictation and SayIt recording work everywhere without asking again.

3

Try it here

Alt+CDictate. Hold while you speak, release to type it.
Alt+ABoard. Opens a see-through board. While it's open, what you dictate goes on the board (and to the clipboard) instead of the page, ready to paste into any app. Press again to close.
Control+Alt+CRead aloud. Reads the selected text, a clicked cell in Google Sheets or Excel, or the text box you're in. Press again to stop.
Alt+RRefine. Fixes spelling, grammar and structure.
Alt+PProfessional. Turns a draft into a polished reply.
Alt+HHumanize. Makes stiff text read naturally.

Change any shortcut in the VoiceWriter window → Hotkeys.

4

SayIt: your voicemail on one key

  1. Click the VoiceWriter icon in the toolbar. In the SayIt card at the top, click Record, say your voicemail, click Stop, listen with Play, then Save.
  2. During a call on Aloware, GoHighLevel or HubSpot, when the voicemail beeps, press Alt+L on any page in Chrome. Your message plays on the call, and you hear it in your headset too. Press it again to stop.
  3. Another dialer? Open it, then VoiceWriter → Settings → SayIt → + Add it.
5

Keep it handy

  • Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin VoiceWriter. Click it any time for SayIt, Read aloud and Settings.
  • VoiceWriter starts working in the tabs you already have open, with no reload. A call that was already going when you installed it gets SayIt from the next call on.
  • Chrome doesn't let extensions into its own pages (such as chrome:// pages and the new tab page) or the Chrome Web Store. There, Alt+A opens the board in a small window instead.
